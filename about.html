About Savanna Brew Café

Brewed in Kenya. Served with love.

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café opened in 2019 with one small espresso machine and a big dream: to celebrate Kenyan coffee.

Founder Amina Otieno grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri and wanted to bring that flavour to the city.

Today the café supports local farmers, uses eco-friendly packaging and hosts community events every month.

"Every cup of coffee should tell a story of passion, community and the beautiful Kenyan land that produced it."

— Amina Otieno, Founder

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do at Savanna Brew Café.

Quality

Only freshly roasted, single-origin Kenyan coffee beans.

Community

Fair prices for farmers and a welcoming space for everyone.

Sustainability

Compostable cups and zero-waste pastries to help protect our environment.

Why Choose Savanna Brew?

We believe great coffee is about more than just a drink. It is about people, culture, hospitality and memorable experiences.
